Rate limiter: a token bucket, the window edge, and the race

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Watch the clock, the counts and the answers. Guess first, then check.

setup

The rule to remember: count in one shared place, and change the count in one step. A token bucket allows a short burst and then a steady speed. A fixed window can let twice the limit through at the edge of the minute. Two servers that read, then write, can both let the last request in.
