Online shop checkout: the last mug, a failed payment, and the race

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Watch the stock, the orders, the seen events and the outbox. Guess first, then check.

setup

The rule to remember: hold the stock with one conditional update (WHERE stock >= 1), zero rows means sold out. The webhook decides that an order is paid, not the browser. Check the signature, save the event id, and move an order only from RESERVED. When something fails, the undo steps run: give the stock back, refund money that came too late.
