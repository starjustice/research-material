Replication and quorums: sync or async, and how many copies must answer

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Watch the copies, the saves and the answers. Guess first, then check.

setup

The rule to remember: waiting for more copies is safer and slower. An asynchronous leader answers first and can lose its newest writes in a crash. A synchronous leader waits and loses none, but each write is slower. With N copies, W saves and R answers, a read always meets a copy that has the newest write when R + W is more than N.
