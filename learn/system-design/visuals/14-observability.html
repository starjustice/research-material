Observability: one slow checkout, one trace id

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Watch the trace id travel, the bars grow, the log lines appear and the numbers change. Guess first, then check.

setup

The rule to remember: metrics say that something is wrong. The trace says where. Logs say what. One trace id, made by the first server and passed on in the traceparent header, ties all three together. The slow step is the span with the biggest own time: its time minus the spans directly under it. All numbers are examples.
