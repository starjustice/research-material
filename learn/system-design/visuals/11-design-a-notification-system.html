Notification system: one message, two lanes, and provider trouble

Press Next and watch one message at a time. The big number is the queue length. Guess first, then check.

setup

The rule to remember: check first (key, preferences, limit), then queue, then send. Give urgent messages their own lane. Retry only errors that can pass, with growing waits. "Sent" only means the provider took the message. A later webhook says "delivered".
