Notification system: one event, a failed send, a retry and a dedupe

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Watch the checks, the notifications table, the queues and what Sari really sees. Guess first, then check.

setup

The rule to remember: the event goes in a queue and the loan service moves on. The notification service checks the dedupe key, the user's choices, quiet hours and limits, then puts one job per channel in that channel's queue. A short problem (429, 500, 503) gets a retry with a growing wait, then the dead letter queue. A dead token (410 from Apple, 404 from Google) is removed and never retried. Urgent jobs go first. A key that is already sent is never sent again.
