Idempotency and retries: a lost answer, a key, and two copies at once

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Watch Ana's app, the key table and the card. Guess first, then check.

setup

The rule to remember: a timeout means "I do not know if it worked". The app makes one key per action and sends it on every try. The server inserts the key as "in progress" before the work, and saves the answer after it. A repeat gets the saved answer. If two copies insert at once, Postgres lets only one win, and the other answers 409.
