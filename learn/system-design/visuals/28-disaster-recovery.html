Disaster recovery: RPO and RTO, replica or backup, async or sync, and a failover

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Guess first, then check. Stories 2 and 3 use numbers from a real test on Postgres 18.3. The clock times in stories 1 and 4 are examples.

setup

The rule to remember: ask for two numbers first. RPO is how much data you can lose. RTO is how long you can be down. A replica copies mistakes, so you also need backups with saved changes. A failover is a chain of steps whose times add up, and the old primary must be fenced.
