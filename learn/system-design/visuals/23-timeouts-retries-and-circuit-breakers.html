Timeouts, retries and circuit breakers: a stuck call, a crowd of retries, and a breaker

The rule to remember: a call you cannot trust needs four things. A timeout, so one stuck call cannot hold a slot for ever. Growing waits with a random part, so retries do not arrive together. A breaker, so you stop calling a provider that is down. A fallback, so the user still gets something. And a retry is only safe when the call is safe to repeat (lesson 13).
