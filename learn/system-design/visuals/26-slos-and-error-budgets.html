SLOs and error budgets: 99.9% in minutes, a bad deploy, and ship or freeze

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Guess first, then check. All times and traffic numbers are example numbers.

setup

The rule to remember: the SLO says how much failure is fine. The error budget is that failure, counted in bad requests or minutes. The burn rate says how fast you are spending it. Page when the budget goes fast and is still going fast, and use what is left to decide whether to ship or to fix.
