Consistency and CAP: a stale read, the fix, and a network split

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Watch the leader, the follower and the copy that is still on its way. Guess first, then check.

setup

The rule to remember: a copy always needs time to travel. During that time a read from the copy is old. You can wait for the copy (slower, fresh) or answer at once (fast, maybe old). A network split makes the same choice harder, because waiting may never end. So you choose per feature: money and last items wait, likes and feeds answer.
