What CapRover does: a deploy, a folder, a health check, a certificate

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Guess first, then check. This is a small model of what CapRover decides. It runs the same parseCaptainDefinition, updateOrder, rollout, routeTable, certsToRenew, survivesRedeploy and restoreReport code as the practice answer in the lesson. Nothing here talks to a real server. The rules come from the CapRover docs and source code, read on 2026-11-19.

setup

The rule to remember: a deploy builds the image, updates the Swarm service and reloads nginx. A folder means stop-first and a short gap. A health check keeps a broken copy from taking over. A certificate is asked for once and renewed by a timer. Backups of your data are yours.
