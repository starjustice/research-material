Docker images and layers: layers, the build cache, and a multi-stage build

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Guess first, then check. Stories 2 and 3 use numbers from a real test on Postgres 18.3. The clock times in stories 1 and 4 are examples.

setup

The rule to remember: an image is a stack of read-only layers, and a container adds one thin layer on top. Docker reuses the layers from the top until the first line that changed, then rebuilds everything after it. So put the lines that change least at the top, and ship only what runs.
