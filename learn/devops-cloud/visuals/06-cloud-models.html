Cloud models: who looks after which layer

Use the buttons below to move one step at a time. Guess first, then check. The layer tables are a model made for this lesson. The ping times in story 4 were measured on 2026-11-14.

setup

The rule to remember: every model answers one question, who looks after which layer. Climbing from on-prem to IaaS, PaaS and SaaS, you look after fewer layers, with less control. Your data is yours in every model. Spread over zones to survive a building failing, and pick the region near your users.
