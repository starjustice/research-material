The rule to remember: your app only keeps numbers. Prometheus pulls them on a timer and stores them. Grafana draws what you ask Prometheus for. An alert rule is a question Prometheus asks again and again, and for makes it wait. Graph a counter with rate(), never raw. Ask for p95, not the average. Page on what users feel.
