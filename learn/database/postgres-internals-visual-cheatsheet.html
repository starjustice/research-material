2 · B+tree: wide, short, linked leaves, TID to the heap

Say this: "Storage is read in pages, so the B+tree makes each node one page and packs hundreds of keys in it. That fanout keeps it 3 to 4 levels deep at tens of millions of rows. Leaves hold (value, TID), are sorted and linked, so it serves equality, ranges and ORDER BY."

4 · MVCC: two versions, two snapshots

Say this: "Readers and writers do not block each other because they look at different row versions. Each transaction reads against a snapshot. Only two writers on the same row wait. The price is dead versions that VACUUM must clean."
