The four-part answer, every time

When you do not know

"I have not worked with that directly. What I know is ... so my best guess is ... because ... I would confirm it by running EXPLAIN / reading the docs on ..." This scores far more than "skip".

Always a number

3 to 4 page reads · 8 KB pages · 37 ms → 0.09 ms · OFFSET touched 900,020 rows, keyset 20 · 80 app connections → 20 real ones · deadlock check after 1 s.

Always a tradeoff

Index: faster reads, slower writes. Replica: more reads, stale data. Cache: speed, invalidation. Shard: scale, no cross-shard joins. SERIALIZABLE: safety, retries.

Always your system

TADA double refund (race → FOR UPDATE → unique event id + ledger) · Prisma N+1 · BullMQ jobs that must be idempotent · three databases split by ownership.
