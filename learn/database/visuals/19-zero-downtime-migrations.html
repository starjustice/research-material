The rule to remember: ask two questions about every ALTER TABLE: how long does it hold the strong lock, and does it rewrite the table? Make the lock short, and do big work in small steps.

    
Words on this page:

    
    
      
  • Rewrite: Postgres writes a new copy of the whole table (a new file).
    • 
      
  • lock_timeout: give up waiting for a lock after a set time, then retry.
    • 
      
  • Batch: a small slice of rows (10,000) changed in its own transaction.
    • 
      
  • Backfill: filling a new column for the old rows.
