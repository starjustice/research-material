The rule to remember: authentication says who you are. Authorization says what you may do. Check it on the server, on every request, and say no unless a rule says yes.
  
    
    
  • Role: a label such as admin or user. The role decides which actions are possible.
    • 
    
  • Permission: one thing a role may do, such as "delete a note".
    • 
    
  • Row-level rule: a rule about which rows you may touch, such as "only your own notes".
    • 
    
  • IDOR: you change an id in the URL and see another person's record, because the server never checked the owner.
    • 
  

  Status codes: 401 = I do not know who you are. 403 = I know who you are, and the answer is no. 404 = not found (also used to hide that something exists).
