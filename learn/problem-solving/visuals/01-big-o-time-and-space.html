The rule to remember: Big O is how the number of steps grows when the input grows. Nested loops multiply (n × n). One loop with a fast lookup adds (n).

    
Words on this page:

    
    
      
  • Step: one small piece of work, like one comparison or one lookup.
    • 
      
  • O(n): the steps grow in line with n: double the input, double the steps.
    • 
      
  • O(n²): the steps grow with n × n: double the input, four times the steps.
    • 
      
  • Set: a collection that answers "is this in here?" in about one step.
