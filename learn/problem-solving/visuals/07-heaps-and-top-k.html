The rule to remember: A min-heap keeps the smallest value on top, and fixes itself in O(log n) after every push or pop. For "top k", keep a heap of size k.

    
Words on this page:

    
    
      
  • Heap: a tree where every parent is smaller than its children (min-heap).
    • 
      
  • Priority queue: a line where the smallest (or biggest) value always leaves first.
    • 
      
  • Bubble up / down: swap a value with its parent / child until the rule holds again.
    • 
      
  • Top k: the k biggest or smallest values.
