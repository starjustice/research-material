The rule to remember: Binary search throws away half of the options at every look. It needs sorted data, or a yes/no check that flips only once.

    
Words on this page:

    
    
      
  • lo and hi: the two ends of the part we still have to look at.
    • 
      
  • mid: the middle of lo and hi. We look here.
    • 
      
  • Sorted: in order from small to big. Binary search needs it.
    • 
      
  • O(log n): the steps grow very slowly: 1,000,000 items need about 20.
